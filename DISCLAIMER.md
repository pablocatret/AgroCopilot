# Responsible use

AgroCopilot is a technical support and demonstration tool. Its responses may
be incomplete, outdated or incorrect.

## Scope

- It does not replace field inspection or the judgement of a qualified
  professional.
- It does not provide regulatory-compliance certification.
- It must not be the sole basis for applying plant-protection products,
  changing irrigation, making investments or taking decisions affecting
  people, animals, crops or the environment.
- Satellite evidence can identify signals and compare periods, but cannot by
  itself establish an agronomic cause.
- Model-cost estimates are indicative and depend on the provider and its
  current pricing.

## Operator responsibility

Operators must:

- cross-check recommendations against field observations and official sources;
- verify labels, authorisations, doses and applicable waiting periods before
  any treatment;
- protect credentials, attachments and farm data;
- review provider settings and the data that may leave the local environment;
- obtain agronomic, veterinary, legal or financial advice when appropriate.

## Data and external providers

Local mode is enabled with `DISABLE_EXTERNALS=1`. When external providers are
enabled, queries and parts of the context may be sent to the configured
services. Operators are responsible for reviewing their terms, privacy
policies and data-protection requirements.
